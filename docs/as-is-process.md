# As-Is Process: Current Manual Procurement Workflow

## Overview

The current procurement process in a typical manufacturing company is **heavily manual**, involving multiple systems, spreadsheets, emails, and phone calls.

---

## Step-by-Step Manual Workflow

### 1. Requirement Creation
- **Who**: Production planner / Department head
- **How**: Fills out a paper form or Excel template
- **Pain**: No visibility into existing stock; duplicate requests common

### 2. Supplier Identification
- **Who**: SCM team
- **How**: Searches paper vendor lists, old purchase records, personal contacts
- **Pain**: Limited to known suppliers; no systematic performance tracking

### 3. RFQ Creation & Distribution
- **Who**: SCM executive
- **How**: Creates RFQ document in Word/Excel, emails to 3–5 suppliers
- **Pain**: Manual formatting, no tracking of who received or responded

### 4. Quotation Collection
- **Who**: SCM executive
- **How**: Collects quotes via email, fax, phone; enters into comparison spreadsheet
- **Pain**: Inconsistent formats, missing data, takes 3–7 days

### 5. Quotation Comparison
- **Who**: SCM analyst
- **How**: Manually creates comparison matrix in Excel
- **Pain**: Error-prone calculations, difficult to normalize different formats

### 6. Manager Approval
- **Who**: Procurement manager
- **How**: Reviews printed comparison, signs physical approval form
- **Pain**: Manager unavailability causes days of delay

### 7. Purchase Order Creation in SAP
- **Who**: SAP operator
- **How**: Manually enters PO details into SAP MM module
- **Pain**: Data re-entry from approved documents, typo risk

### 8. Order Tracking
- **Who**: SCM team
- **How**: Periodic phone/email follow-up with suppliers
- **Pain**: No real-time visibility, delays discovered late

### 9. Goods Receipt / GRN
- **Who**: Warehouse / Quality team
- **How**: Physical inspection, manual GRN entry in SAP
- **Pain**: Paper-based quality reports, delayed data entry

### 10. Inventory Update
- **Who**: Warehouse clerk
- **How**: Manual stock entry, periodic physical counts
- **Pain**: Inventory mismatches, stockouts not detected early

### 11. Invoice Processing
- **Who**: Accounts team
- **How**: Receives paper/PDF invoice, manually matches against PO and GRN
- **Pain**: 3-way matching is tedious, discrepancies require investigation

### 12. Payment Processing
- **Who**: Finance team
- **How**: Batches payments weekly, manual bank transfers
- **Pain**: Missed payment terms, lost early-payment discounts

### 13. Reporting
- **Who**: Management
- **How**: Monthly Excel reports compiled manually from multiple sources
- **Pain**: Stale data, inconsistent metrics, no real-time insight

---

## Summary of Pain Points

| Category | Impact |
|----------|--------|
| Manual data entry | High error rate, slow processing |
| Email-based communication | No tracking, lost messages |
| Paper-based approvals | Days of delay |
| No real-time visibility | Late detection of problems |
| Spreadsheet analytics | Inconsistent, error-prone |
| SAP manual entry | Duplicate effort, typo risk |
